# Roadmap

## Built for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon (September 30 to October 30, 2026)

- Planning agent for anything outside the home, with Qloo's taste at every step: taste profile, heatmap zone, taste-matched search and scoring.
- Hotel lanes with listed prices only, rides between stops, routes on real roads, round trip.
- Booking with approval: links, emails, calls, calendar.
- The Expedition: trips on the real Earth in 3D, with Qloo taste over the city and a plane flying in.
- Real execution: one-tap checkout, wallet prepayments, real AI calls day by day, reroutes to Qloo's next best, receipts and refunds.
- Wallet, identity verification (Persona) and a full traveller record.
- Exports: calendar, PDF, Word, PowerPoint, slides.
- Missions for artist teams built on Qloo audience data.

## Next

1. Photorealistic cities by default (Google Map Tiles key).
2. Identity verification switched on (Persona keys).
3. Calling on a Twilio number for reliable two-way audio; phone numbers verified by code before demo calls.
4. Group taste: blend several people's tastes for a shared plan.
5. Saved plans and sharing a plan with the group.
6. More ride and transit providers by country.
7. Taste over time: learn from the places people pick and skip.
